# IP Registration and Renewal — South African Framework

This overlay covers CIPC as the SA IP registrar, renewal deadlines for trademarks, patents, and designs, the depository patent system, and SA-specific administrative requirements. Loaded by portfolio and cold-start-interview when jurisdiction = ZA.

---

## 1. CIPC as registrar

The **Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)** is the SA government body responsible for the registration and administration of intellectual property rights under the following Acts:

| IP right | Governing Act | Administered by |
|---|---|---|
| Trade marks | Trade Marks Act 194 of 1993 | CIPC — Trade Marks Directorate |
| Patents | Patents Act 57 of 1978 | CIPC — Patents Directorate |
| Designs | Designs Act 195 of 1993 | CIPC — Designs Directorate |
| Copyright | Copyright Act 98 of 1978 | **No registration system** — copyright is automatic |

CIPC is the SA equivalent of both the USPTO (for TM, patent, and design registration) and (partly) the Copyright Office — but SA has no copyright registration, so that function has no equivalent.

## 2. Trademark registration and renewal

### Timeline

| Stage | Typical timeframe |
|---|---|
| Filing | Day 0 |
| Formalities examination | 1–3 months |
| Substantive examination | 4–10 months from filing |
| Acceptance (if no objection) | 8–18 months from filing |
| Advertisement for opposition | 3-month opposition window after acceptance |
| Registration (if no opposition) | 12–24 months from filing (total) |

**Backlog:** CIPC currently has significant examination backlog. Plan for 18 months minimum before registration.

### Renewal deadlines

| Milestone | Rule |
|---|---|
| Registration term | 10 years from the **filing date** (not registration date) |
| Earliest renewal filing | 6 months before the 10-year anniversary of the filing date |
| Renewal deadline | On or before the 10-year anniversary |
| Grace period | 6 months after expiry, with a penalty fee |
| Effect of non-renewal | Registration lapses — mark becomes vulnerable to third-party filing |
| Non-use cancellation | After 5 continuous years of non-use, any person may apply to cancel the registration (TMA s27) |

### Renewal process

- File renewal form at CIPC with the prescribed fee per class
- No re-examination of the mark on renewal — purely administrative
- Renewal is indefinitely repeatable in further 10-year periods
- Ensure the registered address for service is current — CIPC notices go to the address of record

### Nice classification

SA uses the **NICE International Classification of Goods and Services**. Ensure the correct class(es) cover the commercial use case. Covering too narrow a class leaves gaps; covering too many classes increases costs. Multi-class applications: separate fee per class, each class can have different goods/services specifications.

## 3. Patent registration and annual renewal (annuities)

### Examination — depository system

SA operates a **depository patent system**. CIPC conducts **formal examination only** — it checks completeness, formalities, prescribed forms, and classification. It does NOT assess novelty, inventive step, or industrial applicability.

| What CIPC checks | What CIPC does NOT check |
|---|---|
| Completeness of specification | Novelty (prior art search) |
| Correct form and classification | Inventive step |
| Drawings (if required) | Industrial applicability |
| Priority claim formalities | Patentability of the claims |

**A granted SA patent may be invalid.** Validity is tested only in **litigation or revocation proceedings** before the Commissioner of Patents (a High Court judge). Before relying on or licensing a SA patent, conduct a validity assessment.

### Patent term and annuities

| Milestone | Rule |
|---|---|
| Patent term | 20 years from the filing date |
| First annuity due | 3rd anniversary of the filing date |
| Annual annuities | Due on each anniversary (year 3 through year 20) |
| Grace period | Short grace period with surcharge for late payment |
| Lapse | Non-payment causes the patent to lapse |
| Restoration | A lapsed patent may be restored on application — must show unintentional delay |

**Diarising annuities:** Annuity dates run from the SA filing date (or PCT national phase entry date for PCT applications). Diarise all annuity dates from the filing date, not from the grant date.

### PCT applications in SA

SA is a member of the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT). PCT applications designating SA enter national phase at CIPC. The national phase deadline is typically **31 months from the priority date** (same as the general PCT rule). SA local attorneys must be instructed for national phase entry.

## 4. Design registration and renewal

SA has two categories of design registration under the Designs Act 195 of 1993:

| Category | Register | What qualifies | Registrability standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aesthetic design** | Part A | Features of an article judged solely by the eye — appearance, shape, configuration, pattern, ornamentation | New and original |
| **Functional design** | Part F | Features of an article necessitated by the function it performs | New and not commonplace in the art |

### Design terms

| Category | Maximum term | Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetic design | 15 years from filing date | Periodic renewals during the 15-year period |
| Functional design | 10 years from filing date | Periodic renewals during the 10-year period |

**Dual filing recommendation:** Where an article has both aesthetic appeal (distinctive appearance) and functional features, consider filing separate applications for both Part A (aesthetic) and Part F (functional) protection. This maximises coverage.

### Examination

CIPC examines design applications for formalities. Substantive assessment for novelty/originality is also conducted, but enforcement of validity remains a matter for the courts.

## 5. SA IP renewal deadlines — quick reference table

| IP right | Category | Term | First renewal trigger | Grace period |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trademark | All marks | 10 years from filing | 6 months before expiry | 6 months after expiry (with penalty) |
| Patent | All patents | 20 years from filing | Year 3 annuity (3rd filing anniversary) | Short grace with surcharge |
| Design | Aesthetic | 15 years from filing | Periodic during term | Confirm with CIPC |
| Design | Functional | 10 years from filing | Periodic during term | Confirm with CIPC |
| Copyright | All categories | Life+50 (or 50 years from publication) | No renewal required | N/A — no registration |

## 6. CIPC administration — practical notes

- **Online portal:** CIPC provides an online portal for certain TM and patent filings and searches. Not all transactions are available online; some require paper or agent filing.
- **Registered attorneys:** Only persons admitted as patent attorneys (Patents Act s19) may act before CIPC in patent matters. Trademark and design applications can be filed by the owner or by an attorney or IP practitioner.
- **Address of record:** CIPC sends all notices and correspondence to the registered address of record (the applicant or agent). Keep address details current to avoid missed deadlines.
- **CIPC search tools:** CIPC provides online trademark, patent, and design search tools. These are useful for clearance but are not always comprehensive or up-to-date. A professional search through a qualified IP practitioner is recommended for important clearance decisions.

## 7. What NOT to replicate from US practice

| US concept | Why it does not apply in SA |
|---|---|
| **§8 declaration (Trademark Maintenance)** | US trademark registrations require a §8 declaration of use between years 5-6 and again at each renewal. SA has no equivalent maintenance filing — renewal is administrative only (fee payment, no use declaration). |
| **§8/§9 affidavit of use / non-use** | US TM maintenance mechanism. SA has no equivalent. Non-use is addressed via s27 TMA cancellation (an adversarial proceeding, not a maintenance filing). |
| **USPTO TSDR (Trademark Status and Document Retrieval)** | US online prosecution history and status system. SA equivalent is the CIPC online portal, which has more limited functionality. |
| **US design patent (35 USC §171)** | US design patents have a single category and a 15-year term (since 2015). SA has two categories (aesthetic 15yr, functional 10yr) under the Designs Act — distinct from the US design patent regime. |
| **Substantive patent examination** | SA's depository system means no substantive examination occurs at CIPC. Do not assume a granted SA patent has been vetted for novelty or inventive step — it has not. |
| **US copyright registration** | SA has no copyright registration system. Copyright is automatic. Do not advise SA clients that they must register to enforce their copyright. |
| **USPTO Patent Center** | US online patent prosecution system. SA uses CIPC's online filing portal — very different in scope and functionality. |
